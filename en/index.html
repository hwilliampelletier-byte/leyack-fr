Painter · Orléans

Le Yack, painter in Orléans

Alexandre Pelletier, known as Le Yack, paints in oil in Orléans, France. A Fine Arts graduate, he spent nearly thirty years composing images as an art director before returning to the canvas. He paints suspended scenes: a petrol station at night, a phone box in the rain, a bedroom where the light comes in at a slant.

His path

From art school to the Atelier Joconde

His full story

  1. The beginnings

    Art school

    The son of a painter, he trained at the École des Beaux-Arts in Orléans: observational drawing, perspective, oil and glazes.

  2. Thirty years

    Art director

    In publishing, he composed images for others. Graphic designer, illustrator, videographer: this is where his name was born.

  3. After Covid

    Back to the canvas

    He reopened his boxes of drawings and painted some thirty canvases in a year for his first exhibition, at L’Empreinte Galerie.

  4. Today

    The Atelier Joconde

    He paints in oil in Orléans, in the studio he shares with his two sons, and exhibits at art fairs across Europe.

Family

The Atelier Joconde, a father and his two sons
